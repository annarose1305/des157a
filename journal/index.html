Best practices for form design

Reading through the article, I understand how important it is when considering the designs of the forms even when they are simple. One of the consistent things that I notice is how all of these practices are related to making it an easier process to fill out forms. It is pretty important to make it an easy process, as there have been times where I would back out of a form because it seemed too overwhelming.

Another important aspect that stood out was the concept of the feedback from forms. In my experience, it has been frustrating when I didn’t see the option to view the password I put in or if I submitted it. Feedback has also been helpful when there are certain items of the form that I’m missing. To expand on this, I’d rather be directed to the area where I’m missing something rather than having to go back to look for it myself. Overall, these practices suggested by the article are helpful to keep in mind when making a good form experience for the users.

Instagram

I would say that Instagram does exemplify best practices because it keeps it short and simple when signing up for an account. In particular, it tells users why they want particular information such as contact information and birthday, ensuring that users know why they're being asked.
